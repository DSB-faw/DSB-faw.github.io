The Story

Every dataset has a backstory.

Mine starts on a support queue, not a dashboard. Three chapters — Zomato, IIM Lucknow, Cars24 — each one answering the same question a little more rigorously: why did the user actually leave?

01
Jul 2019 – Oct 2021 · Zomato (Eternal), Gurgaon
Where the funnel started as a phone call

Deepak's first seat was a Customer Delight Executive role on Zomato's Food@Work vertical — resolving 50–60 tickets a day, ~5 minutes at a time. That frontline view taught him something most analysts never see directly: the exact moment a conversion turns into a complaint. Promoted to Team Lead over 7 agents, he started treating support like a product surface — assigning vertical ownership by individual strength, and building automation that closed feedback loops instead of just logging them.

  • UAT lead for product feature rollouts — partnered with PMs on launch readiness, edge-case validation, and post-launch triage.
  • Launched Food@Work at the Infosys campus mid-COVID, introducing pre-booked, distanced table reservations as a support-side answer to an ops constraint.
  • Built Freshdesk keyword-matching automation that auto-tagged tickets and routed feedback-only cases straight to vendors and cafe managers — no manual triage.
02
2022 · IIM Lucknow, Executive Programme
Naming the instinct

Two-plus years of solving problems by feel led Deepak looking for the framework underneath. IIM Lucknow's Executive Programme in Data-Driven Product Management gave those instincts a vocabulary — hypothesis design, statistical rigor, KPI trees — turning "I think this is why users drop off" into something that could be tested, measured, and defended in a room full of stakeholders.

03
Oct 2021 – Present · Cars24 Australia, Gurgaon
Where the model meets the market

At Cars24, the same question got a much bigger canvas: a marketplace with real acquisition spend on the line. As Data Analytics Manager, Deepak rebuilt how leads get scored, attributed, and acted on — end to end.

  • Built an XGBoost intent-scoring model that let sales focus on leads likely to convert — cutting the priority queue 9% while holding conversion flat.
  • Ran structured A/B tests on Test Drive Ops for weekend-preferred users, lifting Lead-to-Test-Booking conversion 15%.
  • Rebuilt First-Touch attribution from scratch and found 10% of "Product" credit actually belonged to Marketplace — spend got redirected to the real growth driver.
  • Paired the corrected attribution with a listing-optimization engine to pull CAC down 27% (110 AUD → 80 AUD) across a 3,500-lead base.
  • Shipped an AI chatbot for off-hours leads (+2pp booking uplift, now piloting in working hours) and a Python + Google Maps geocoding pipeline that standardized addresses across 3,500+ leads.
  • Built a Snowflake + Looker QA platform that randomized call audits across 25 agents, lifting call quality 20% — and caught a ~₹1M conversion risk buried in a CRM restructuring plan before it shipped.
